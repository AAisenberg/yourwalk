Methodology note

Higher score = better walking conditions. Confidence is data trust, not walk quality. Missing general crossings, kerb ramps, and tactiles reduce confidence rather than being scored as absent.

Sources behind this view

Segment network: Casey Footpaths (T1EAM), including shared-use path class.
Accessibility: width, surface, speed zones, graffiti proxy, school crossing bonus.
Day: Urban Heat 2018, Vicmap tree density, fountains, benches.
Night: street + park lights, night pedestrian crashes.
